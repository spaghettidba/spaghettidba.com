I won’t repeat all <a href="/2011/07/29/exceptional-dba-awards-2011/">the reasons why I think that Jeff deserves the award</a>, I just want to say that I could not agree more with this result.

<ul>
	<li>a trophy at <a href="http://www.sqlpass.org/summit/2011/">PASS Summit 2011</a></li>
	<li>a full conference registration for theSummit</li>
	<li>four nights’ accommodations and $300 in travel expenses</li>
	<li>a copy of Red Gate’s <a href="http://www.red-gate.com/products/SQL_Professional_Toolbelt/index.htm">SQL Toolbelt</a></li>
</ul>
Congratulations Jeff, well deserved.
